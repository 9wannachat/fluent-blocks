description: Author and review bilingual lessons, practice items and reusable English patterns for Fluent Blocks.
---

Use the lesson contract and approved roadmap in docs/SPEC.md and docs/PROGRESS.md.
Start from what the learner wants to express. Explain the choice in Thai, show a contrast and apply it to original speech.
Include five worked examples, five scored items, contrasts, a brief dialogue, common mistakes and a speaking task.
Give a pattern two natural contexts and constrain its slots. Document valid variants, conversational register and dialect as relevant.
Read every translation and answer aloud for language review. Run npm run validate:content and report any incomplete editorial review.
